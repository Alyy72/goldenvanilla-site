01 — Strength

High vanillin content

Ugandan beans are prized for naturally high vanillin — the compound that carries vanilla flavor. More aroma per gram means your recipes need less bean to hit the same intensity.

02 — Texture

Oily, plump pods

Grade A gourmet beans arrive supple and glossy, heavy with caviar. They split clean, scrape generously, and infuse fast — no brittle, dried-out pods from a distributor's shelf.

03 — Profile

Bold & chocolatey

A deep, cocoa-leaning flavor that punches through the acidity of a double espresso or a rich ganache — where mild, floral Madagascar vanilla quietly disappears.
